## Overview

Velza Gallery is a Persian-language, right-to-left jewelry retail website with a dark-and-gold visual identity, product photography, and editorial sections to showcase gold and jewelry collections.

The supplied full-page homepage screenshot (31 October 2026) documents:

- A prominent editorial hero with a primary shopping call to action
- A best-selling products section featuring jewelry imagery and displayed prices
- Category-led navigation for necklaces, earrings, and other jewelry
- Collection and jewelry-workshop promotional sections
- A new-products grid and craftsmanship-focused editorial content
